## v1.11.0

- Gold found inside pickpocketed lockboxes and junkboxes now counts toward your haul and lifetime totals when you open them. Boxes pickpocketed before this update are not recognised.
- New option: Count Lockbox Gold (on by default) — Options → Display, or /pp lockbox on|off

## v1.10.0

- New option: show money as text (5g 20s 30c) instead of coin icons — Options → Display, or /pp money text|icons
